Datetime/range input used in report forms. The webapp attribute encodes the picker mode: explicit_<begin>_<end>, since_<date>, past_<N>_<unit>, current_<N>_<unit>.

Explicit range — 2021-12-01 → 2021-12-15

Since — anchored at 2021-12-01 to now

Current week — rolling 1 week including today

Past 2 days — rolling, ending now

Error — invalid service (current_99_week exceeds bounds)
