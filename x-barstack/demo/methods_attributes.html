Attributes
machine-id
Id of the machine, forwarded to every bar.
period-context
Page used to read the configuration. Forwarded to every bar.
main-bar
reason, running or production: fixed main bar. When missing, the main bar depends on the configuration.
range
Displayed range, forwarded to every bar.
machine-context
Forwarded to every bar.
motion-context
Forwarded to the reason, running and production state bars only.
when
Position of the time selection line, forwarded to x-bartimeselection only.
datetime-context
Forwarded to x-bartimeselection only.
mainbar-*
Forwarded to the main bar without the mainbar- prefix (ex: mainbar-click becomes click).
